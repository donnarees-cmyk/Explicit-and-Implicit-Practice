1️⃣ Warm-Up: Be a Detective 2 min

Look at this scene:

🥾 Muddy boots by the tent door. 🌧️ A dripping raincoat on a hook. 📓 A notebook full of fresh notes. 😁 A big smile on the explorer's face.

Nobody TOLD you what happened today. But can you figure it out? What do you think happened? How do you know?

2️⃣ Learn It: Two Kinds of Information 4 min

🔑 EXPLICIT

The text says it directly. You can put your finger on the words.

💭 Think: "It's right there!"

❓ Question clues: who, what, when, where, how many

🗝️ IMPLICIT

The text hints at it but does not say it. You figure it out by making an inference.

💭 Think: "Read between the lines."

❓ Question clues: why, how do you know, what can you infer, what does this suggest

An inference is a smart guess you make with clues from the text and what you already know.

🔎 Text Clues ➕ 🧠 What I Know 🟰 💡 Inference

🔒 Locksmith tip: Explicit information is an open door, so you just walk in. Implicit information is a locked door, and the text clues are your key!

3️⃣ Watch Me: Think-Aloud 3 min

Leo slammed his locker shut. He stomped down the hallway, dropped into his seat, and crossed his arms. When his friend said, "Hi," Leo stared at his desk and didn't answer.
🔑 Explicit: Leo slammed his locker. He did not answer his friend. I can point right to those words.
🗝️ Implicit: Leo is upset or angry.
🔎 Text clues: slammed, stomped, crossed his arms, didn't answer.
🧠 What I know: People often act this way when they are mad or having a bad day.
💡 Inference: Leo is upset about something. The text never says "angry," but the clues unlock it!

Field Notes from the Forest Edge

¶1Maya pulled her hood tighter as rain dripped off the leaves. She had been sitting on the same fallen log for three hours, and the pages of her notebook were starting to curl. On her first day, her mentor, Dr. Okafor, had told her, "The forest decides when you are ready to see it."

¶2Across the clearing, a young chimpanzee peeked out from behind its mother's back. Maya kept her eyes low and her hands still. She did not reach for her camera, even though it was right beside her.

¶3The mother chimp grunted softly and went back to cracking nuts with a flat stone. The young one crept forward, one careful step at a time, then plopped down in the mud only a few yards from Maya's boots. It tilted its head and stared at her.

¶4Maya's heart pounded so hard she was sure the whole forest could hear it. Slowly, she lifted her pencil and wrote: Day 9. Young chimp came within 5 meters. Mother calm. Using a stone tool on nuts.

¶5When the rain stopped, the chimps melted back into the trees. Maya stood, stretched her stiff legs, and grinned all the way back to camp. Dr. Okafor took one look at her muddy boots and her smiling face. "Well?" he asked. "Did the forest decide?"

¶6Maya just held up her notebook.
